📖 Glossary

What every term in this dashboard means.
Total Net Wealth
Everything you own inside Trade Republic right now, at current market prices. Sum of all positions (stocks, ETFs, bonds, crypto, private equity) plus your cash balance.
Brokerage / Bonds / Private Equity / Crypto
Different "buckets" that Trade Republic uses internally. The official mobile app shows each as a separate tile. Brokerage covers stocks and ETFs — usually the biggest one.
Deposits
Money you sent FROM your own bank account TO Trade Republic. It's still your money, just inside the broker now ready to invest.
Withdrawals
The opposite: money you sent FROM Trade Republic BACK TO your own bank. Reduces "net capital in TR".
Card spending (a.k.a. Removals)
Money that left your TR cash balance via the Trade Republic card (lifestyle consumption — coffee, groceries, restaurants). Different from Withdrawals because it didn't go back to your bank, it was spent.
Tax refunds
When Trade Republic recovers tax that was over-withheld on a dividend, it credits the difference back to you. Counts as money "in".
Net capital in TR
Deposits + Tax refunds − Withdrawals. The money you've committed to Trade Republic for investing. Card spending is NOT subtracted because it's lifestyle, not "moving money out for investing purposes".
Current value
Today's portfolio + cash, valued at live market prices. The number you see in "Total Net Wealth" at the top.
Lifetime P/L
Current value + Card spending − Net capital in TR − Investment income. The pure price appreciation on the capital you've committed.
Investment income
Dividends from stocks/ETFs, plus interest TR paid you on your cash balance. Not counted in P/L because it's income, not appreciation.
Top Paying Issuers
Ranked list of which companies/ETFs paid you the most in dividends over your account's history. Useful for seeing where your dividend income concentrates.
Payment Ledger
Searchable list of every individual dividend and interest payment received, with date, issuer, ISIN, type, and amount.
ISIN
International Securities Identification Number. The global unique ID for a stock, ETF, bond, etc. Two letters of country + 10 digits/letters.
Net traded
Total stock purchases − Total stock sales. The money you've parked in positions.
Asset Allocation
How your wealth is split across different asset types. Diversification metric.
Concentration warnings
Heuristic alerts: "Top-1 position is X% of depot", "Top-5 are X%", etc. Helps notice over-concentration in one stock or class.
Session keepalive
Background process that refreshes your Trade Republic session every ~290 seconds so it doesn't expire (TR sessions die after 5-15 min idle otherwise).
